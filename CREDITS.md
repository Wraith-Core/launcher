# Wraith Core launcher: credits

Everything the launcher ships besides its own code, with where it came from and its licence. The look is the
server's own interface (w_lib theme, w_loading, w_pausemenu); only material that may be redistributed publicly
is included. The game's own typefaces (RDR Lino, RDR Catalogue, Hapna Slab) and Rockstar's UI textures are
**not** shipped: the wordmark uses EB Garamond instead.

## Survey plates (`renderer/plates/`, from w_loading)

Each plate was cropped inside the print, so no mount board, handwritten captions or publisher imprints remain. All seven were then graded with one recipe: automatic levels and exposure, a gentle S-curve, and a warm albumen monochrome ramp (shadows `#14110D`, mids `#6E6557`, highlights `#E4DCCB`). Each was saved as a 1920x1080 progressive JPEG at q80 (`hanging_rock.jpg` at q78, the step that keeps it under 500 KB). Their captions are the `screen.plates.<id>.*` keys in `locales/`.

## Music (`renderer/music/`, from w_loading)

The page plays the three tracks in order, 01, 02, 03, then loops, with a 2.5 s crossfade between them. The default volume is 0.25. All three were trimmed and loudness-matched to -18 LUFS, then re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis. None of them legally requires attribution, but each is credited here.

## Typefaces (`renderer/theme/fonts/`)

The same files as the server theme (`w_lib/nui/theme/fonts`), unmodified Google Fonts subsets, all under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1 (licence texts next to the files): Lalezar, Amiri, Aref Ruqaa, Tajawal,
EB Garamond, Noto Sans Arabic.

## Textures (`renderer/tex/`)

`tear.png` (the torn-edge 9-slice mask) and `grain.png` (the paper grain) are w_pausemenu's own procedural
textures, generated by `w_pausemenu/web/tools/gen-textures.mjs`.
